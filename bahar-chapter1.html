Bahar-e-Shariat - Chapter 1

Taharat (Purification)

Introduction

Taharat yani paaki Islam ka bunyadi aur zaroori hissa hai. Har ibadat se pehle paak hona farz hai.

Purity (Taharat) is the foundation of Islamic practice. Before offering any act of worship, physical and spiritual cleanliness is required.

1. Paaki ki Iqsam (Types of Purity)

Roman Urdu: Paaki do qisam ki hoti hai: Haqiqi aur Hukmi.

English: There are two types of purity: actual (physical) and legal (ritual).

2. Wuzu aur Ghusl ke Farz

Roman Urdu: Wuzu ke 4 farz hain: chehra dhona, dono haathon ko kohniyon tak dhona, sir ka masah, dono paon dhona.

English: The four obligatory acts of ablution (Wudu) are: washing the face, washing arms up to elbows, wiping the head, and washing feet.

3. Najasat se Paaki

Roman Urdu: Najasat ko do qisam mein taqseem kiya gaya hai: Najasat-e-Haqiqi aur Najasat-e-Hukmi.

English: Impurity is categorized into two types: real and legal impurity.
