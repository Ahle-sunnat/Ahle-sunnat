Chapter 3: Janaza (Funeral Rites)

Janaza ki Ahmiyat

Islam mein mayyit ke izzat aur uske huqooq ki badi ahmiyat hai. Janaza ki tayyari, Namaz-e-Janaza, aur dafn ke aadaab mukammal tareeqe se bayan kiye gaye hain.

Ghusl-e-Mayyit

Mayyit ko dafn se pehle Ghusl diya jata hai. Yeh ek Farz-e-Kifayah hai. Ghusl ke liye teen baar paani se dho kar agar mumkin ho toh kaafur milaya jata hai.

Kafan

Mard ke liye teen kapron ka kafan aur aurat ke liye paanch kapdon ka kafan hota hai. Kafan saaf aur paak hona chahiye.

Namaz-e-Janaza

Namaz-e-Janaza Farz-e-Kifayah hai. Isme chaar takbeerein hoti hain. Ye Namaz duaon par mabni hoti hai jisme mayyit ke liye maghfirat ki dua ki jati hai.

Dafn aur Qabar ke Aadaab

Dafn ke waqt qibla ki taraf mooh karke mayyit ko leṭhaya jata hai. Qabar mein pehle Surah Fatiha aur kuch duaayein padhi jati hain.
