About Ahlus Sunnah

English:
We follow the path of Prophet Muhammad (Peace Be Upon Him), his noble family (Ahl-e-Bait), righteous companions (Sahaba), and the pious saints (Awliya Allah). Our way is the way of love, respect, and reverence.

Roman Urdu:
Hum Nabi Muhammad Mustafa (SAW), Ahl-e-Bait, Sahaba-e-Kiram aur Awliya Allah ke tareeqe par chalte hain. Hamara rasta mohabbat, adab aur buzurgon ki ta'zeem ka rasta hai.
