Daily Hadith

Hadith 1: Authenticity of Prophet's (SAW) message

Arabic:
"إنما الأعمال بالنيات، وإنما لكل امرئ ما نوى..."
Translation:
"Actions are judged by intentions, and everyone will be rewarded according to what they intended." (Sahih Bukhari)
Roman Urdu:
"Aamal ka ta'alluq niyat se hai, aur har shakhs ko uski niyat ke mutabiq inaam milega." (Sahih Bukhari)

Hadith 2: The Importance of Knowledge

Arabic:
"طلب العلم فريضة على كل مسلم..."
Translation:
"Seeking knowledge is obligatory upon every Muslim." (Ibn Majah)
Roman Urdu:
"Ilm haasil karna har Musalman par farz hai." (Ibn Majah)

Hadith 3: Love for Prophet Muhammad (SAW)

Arabic:
"لا يؤمن أحدكم حتى أكون أحب إليه من والده وولده والناس أجمعين..."
Translation:
"None of you truly believes until I am more beloved to him than his father, his children, and all of mankind." (Sahih Bukhari)
Roman Urdu:
"Tum mein se koi bhi us waqt tak imaan nahi lata jab tak main uske liye uske walid, uske bachon aur saari duniya se zyada pyara na hoon." (Sahih Bukhari)

Hadith 4: The Power of Duaa

Arabic:
"الدعاء هو العبادة..."
Translation:
"Supplication is the essence of worship." (Tirmidhi)
Roman Urdu:
"Dua ibadat ka asal hai." (Tirmidhi)
