Chapter 1: Fiqh ka Ta'aruf

Roman Urdu: Fiqh Islam ka ek ahem hissa hai jo shari'at ke masa'il ka ilmi tajziya karta hai. Ye Qur'an aur Hadith se masail ka hukm nikalta hai.

English Translation: Fiqh is an important branch of Islamic knowledge which deals with legal rulings derived from the Qur’an and Hadith.

Imam Ahmad Raza Khan (RA) ne is chapter mein fiqh ka mukammal ta'aruf diya hai aur uski buniyad Qur’an o Sunnat par rakhi hai.
