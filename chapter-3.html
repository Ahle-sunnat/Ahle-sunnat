Chapter 3: Islamic Family Law

Roman Urdu: Shadi, talaaq, meher aur waris ke qawaneen Islamic Family Law ka hissa hain, jo Qur’an aur Sunnat se sabit hain.

English Translation: Marriage, divorce, dower (mehr), and inheritance laws are integral to Islamic Family Law based on the Qur’an and Sunnah.
