Chapter 6: Islamic Aadaab aur Akhlaaq

Roman Urdu: Islam mein ikhlaaqi taleemat aur insani aadaab par bohot zor diya gaya hai. Imam Ahmad Raza ne sunnat ke roshni mein inhein wazahat di.

English Translation: Islam emphasizes good character and manners. Imam Ahmad Raza Khan has beautifully explained these in the light of Sunnah.
