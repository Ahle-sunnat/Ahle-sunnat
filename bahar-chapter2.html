Chapter 2: Namaz (Salah)

Namaz Ki Ahmiyat (Importance of Salah)

Namaz Islam ka doosra rukun hai. Qur’an aur Hadith mai iski bohat zyada ahmiyat bayan ki gayi hai. Har baaligh Muslim par farz hai ke wo rozana paanch waqt ki Namaz ada kare.

Farz, Wajib, Sunnat, Aur Nafl Namaz

Farz: Paanch waqt ki Namaz (Fajr, Zuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha)
Wajib: Witr, Eid ki Namazen
Sunnat: Sunnat-e-Muakkadah aur Ghair Muakkadah
Nafl: Tahajjud, Ishraq, Chasht, etc.

Namaz Ke Arkaan (Essential Elements of Salah)

Takbeer-e-Tahreema, Qiyam, Qira'at, Ruku, Sujood, Qa'dah, Salam — ye sab arkaan hai jo baghair namaz ada nahi hoti.

Shara’it (Conditions) of Namaz

Taharat (cleanliness), Kapron aur Jisam ka paak hona, Qibla ki taraf munh, waqt ka aana, niyyat, etc.
