Chapter 2: Islamic Jurisprudence ke Usool

Roman Urdu: Fiqhi Usool woh qawaid hain jin ki buniyad par masail ka hukm nikalta hai. Inmein Qur’an, Hadith, Ijma aur Qiyas shamil hain.

English Translation: Principles of Islamic Jurisprudence include the foundational sources like Qur’an, Hadith, Consensus (Ijma), and Analogical Reasoning (Qiyas).
