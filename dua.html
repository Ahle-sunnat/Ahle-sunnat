Daily Dua

Dua 1: Dua for Protection

Arabic:
"اللهم إني أعوذ بك من فتنة النار وفتنة القبر وفتنة المسيح الدجال..."
Translation:
"O Allah, I seek refuge with You from the trial of the Fire, the trial of the grave, and the trial of the Dajjal." (Sahih Muslim)
Roman Urdu:
"Allahumma inni a'udhu bika min fitnatil naar wa fitnatil qabr wa fitnatil Masiha ad-Dajjal." (Sahih Muslim)

Dua 2: Dua for Seeking Forgiveness

Arabic:
"اللهم اغفر لي وارحمني..."
Translation:
"O Allah, forgive me and have mercy on me." (Sahih Bukhari)
Roman Urdu:
"Allahumma ghfir li warhamni." (Sahih Bukhari)

Dua 3: Dua for Guidance

Arabic:
"اللهم إني أسالك الهداية..."
Translation:
"O Allah, I ask You for guidance, piety, chastity, and independence." (Sahih Muslim)
Roman Urdu:
"Allahumma inni as'alu-ka al-hidaya, wa at-tuqaa, wal-‘afafa, wal-ghinaa." (Sahih Muslim)

Dua 4: Dua for Relief from Distress

Arabic:
"اللهم إني عبدك ابن عبدك..."
Translation:
"O Allah, I am Your servant, the son of Your servant..." (Sahih Muslim)
Roman Urdu:
"Allahumma inni 'abdu-ka ibn 'abdi-ka..." (Sahih Muslim)
